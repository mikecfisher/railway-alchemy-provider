# Code of conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, constructive, and harassment-free community.
Participants should:

- be respectful and assume good intent;
- critique ideas and code rather than people;
- accept corrections and acknowledge tradeoffs;
- protect credentials and private operational information;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or unwanted personal attention.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this standard and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation for repeated or serious
violations.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile.
Do not use a public issue when a report contains sensitive personal details.

Maintainers will handle reports as confidentially as practical and will avoid
conflicts of interest when reviewing them.
